HERITAGE ORIGIN

From the Heart of Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is home to one of the world's most distinctive varieties of cinnamon — authentic Ceylon Cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum).

Grown within the island's tropical climate and nurtured by generations of traditional knowledge, Ceylon Cinnamon carries a delicate sweet aroma, naturally warm character, and subtle refined flavor that sets it apart from all common cassia.

From green plantations and mist-covered hills to coastal landscapes, the spirit of Sri Lanka lives within every carefully crafted cinnamon stick.

DAILY RITUAL

A Simple Ritual. A Distinctive Taste.

01

ONE STICK

Place one cinnamon infusion stick into your ceramic cup.

02

200ml HOT WATER

Pour freshly boiled water over the infusion stick.

03

INFUSE

Allow the natural cinnamon to infuse gently for 3-5 minutes.

04

STIR & ENJOY

Stir gently to unlock the full warm bouquet of Ceylon.
